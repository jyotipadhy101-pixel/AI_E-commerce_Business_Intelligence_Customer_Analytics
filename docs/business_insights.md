# Business Insights & Recommendations

## Key Insights

- Monitor **Sales, Profit, and Profit Margin** together to evaluate overall performance.
- Analyze **category and regional performance** to identify business opportunities.
- Review **high-discount transactions** to understand their impact on profitability.
- Use **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)** and **Average Order Value (AOV)** to measure customer value.
- Apply **RFM segmentation** to identify Champions, Loyal, At Risk, and Lost customers.
- Identify **high-value customers at higher churn risk** using AI-based predictions.

## Recommended Actions

- Optimize discount strategies while maintaining profitability.
- Personalize customer engagement using RFM segments.
- Prioritize retention strategies for valuable high-risk customers.
- Monitor sales, profitability, customer, and churn KPIs through Power BI.

## Key KPIs

**Sales:** Total Sales · Monthly Sales  
**Profitability:** Total Profit · Profit Margin  
**Customers:** CLV · AOV  
**RFM:** Customer Segment  
**Churn:** Churn Probability · Risk Level

## Conclusion

The analysis combines **Sales Analytics, Profitability Analysis, Customer Intelligence, RFM Segmentation, and AI Churn Prediction** to support data-driven e-commerce decisions.
