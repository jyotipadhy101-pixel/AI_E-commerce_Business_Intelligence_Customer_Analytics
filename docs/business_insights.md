E-Commerce Sales Analysis — Business Insights

1. Technology Is the Most Profitable Category

Technology generated approximately 836K in sales and 145K in profit, with the highest category profit margin of approximately 17.40%.

Recommendation: Continue focusing on high-performing Technology products and identify opportunities to increase sales while maintaining strong margins.

2. Furniture Has a Low Profit Margin

Furniture generated substantial sales but achieved a profit margin of only approximately 2.49%, significantly below Technology and Office Supplies.

Recommendation: Review Furniture pricing, discount levels, shipping costs, and loss-making products to improve profitability.

3. Copiers Are Highly Profitable

Copiers generated approximately 149.5K in sales and 55.6K in profit, with a profit margin of approximately 37.2%.

Recommendation: Prioritize high-margin products such as Copiers through better inventory planning and targeted promotions.

4. High Discounts Are Hurting Profitability

Transactions with no discount generated approximately 321K in profit, while low-discount transactions generated approximately 101K in profit.

Medium-discount transactions resulted in approximately 35.8K in losses, while high-discount transactions resulted in approximately 99.6K in losses.

Recommendation: Reduce excessive discounting and establish discount limits based on product and category profitability.

5. Consumer Is the Largest Customer Segment

The Consumer segment generated approximately 1.16M in sales, 134K in profit, and 2,586 orders, making it the largest customer segment.

Recommendation: Continue focusing on Consumer customers while developing targeted strategies to increase Corporate and Home Office sales.

6. Many Products Are Generating Losses

The SQL analysis identified 299 products with negative overall profit.

Recommendation: Review these products for excessive discounts, pricing problems, shipping costs, and weak demand. Persistently unprofitable products should be repriced or reconsidered.

7. Top Customers Contribute Significant Sales

Sean Miller ranked first with approximately 25K in sales, followed by Tamara Chand with approximately 19K and Raymond Buch with approximately 15K.

Recommendation: Use customer retention strategies, loyalty programs, and personalized offers to retain high-value customers.

Conclusion

The analysis shows that high sales do not always translate into high profitability. Technology and Copiers are strong performers, while Furniture, excessive discounts, and loss-making products require attention. The business should focus on profitable categories and customer segments while improving discount and product profitability strategies.
